Frequently asked questions.

Do both partners need to pay? +
No. One LoveLynk subscription covers both partners. When you subscribe and connect your partner, their account is included at no additional cost.
How much does LoveLynk cost? +
LoveLynk is £4.99/month or £19.99/year for a couple, with a 7-day free trial. That is around £1.67/month per couple on the yearly plan, or £0.83 per person. Your app marketplace will show the terms before you subscribe.
Can I put widgets on my Lock Screen? +
LoveLynk is designed around Home Screen widgets, with compatible Lock Screen widgets as well. The number, size and functionality of Lock Screen widgets vary by device and operating system.
What if we're in different time zones? +
That's exactly what LoveLynk is for. Widgets such as Partner Time, Partner Weather, Distance and Love Compass help you feel in sync even if you're hours or miles apart.
What can I send to my partner? +
The interactive widget collection includes Heartbeat, Kiss and Emoji. They're simple, thoughtful ways to send a little reminder that you're thinking of each other.
Is LoveLynk on iPhone and Android? +
LoveLynk is built for iPhone and Android. Choose your device and follow the store link to install the app. Widgets and interactive features may vary by platform and operating system.
