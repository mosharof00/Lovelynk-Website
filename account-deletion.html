How to request deletion

  1. Open our account-deletion request form.
  2. Choose Account deletion request and provide the email address associated with your LoveLynk account.
  3. We may ask you to verify account ownership before proceeding and will explain any information that must be retained for legal reasons.

If a deletion control is available in your version of the LoveLynk app, you can also use that option. Removing the app from your phone is not the same as deleting the account.

Subscriptions: Deleting an account does not automatically end an Apple or Google subscription. You must separately cancel the subscription in your relevant app store account.
